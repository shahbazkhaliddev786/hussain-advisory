# Surface brief: homepage and Knowledge Hub

Scope: the PASA homepage (Persuade) and Knowledge Hub reading pages (Read). Product truth lives in `PRODUCT.md`.

- **Audience:** public officials, procurement staff and donor-project teams; newcomers learning procurement.
- **Job:** within a few seconds, show who PASA is, what PASA does and why to stay. Primary actions: explore services, request advisory support, view training.
- **Proof on hand:** Naeem Abbas's 30+ years, two decades of training, experience on projects financed by eight institutions, and the Knowledge Hub itself. No testimonials, statistics or client logos exist; none are invented.
- **Constraints:** the logo on a light ground; the brief's section order; the required wording rules in `PRODUCT.md`.

## Directions on the board

The rut is the corporate consulting template: navy header, laptop or handshake stock photo, a grid of icon cards, a strip of funder logos, a testimonial slider. Its predictable opposites, a trendy startup gloss or a cream-and-serif editorial page, are ruts too. The three directions differ in what leads the first viewport.

| | Leads with | World | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. The procurement line** (recommended) | the process | The cycle drawn as one line through the site; bidding-document rigor; navy field, yellow "you are here" leaf | Must keep stage copy tight or the hero reads like a training slide |
| **B. The workbook** | the knowledge | A well-made training handout: margin notes, key-term definitions, highlighter yellow, a hyper-legible face | Can feel like e-learning; less gravitas for advisory buyers |
| **C. Institutional, done properly** | the person | The category standard at full craft: portrait-led, serif headlines, leaf-shaped portrait frame | Familiar; depends on the photograph |

## Direction contract (A, proposed)

THESIS: The procurement cycle is the page's structure. The first viewport draws Plan, Bid, Evaluate, Award, Manage and Close as one line and shows what PASA does at each stage. It refuses the stock-photo hero and the icon-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: A navy (#0B3459) field like a bidding-document cover, white paper below, the logo's yellow leaf (#FCDE7E) as the single "you are here" marker everywhere (cycle, alphabet, navigation). Archivo at expanded widths for headings, Archivo for interface, Source Serif 4 for reading. Hairline rules, near-square corners, the leaf shape reserved for markers and the portrait.

STORY: The visitor sees the whole cycle, finds the stage they're working on, learns that one experienced advisor covers all of it, then requests support, views training, or reads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White header with logo, six navigation items and "Request advisory support". The navy field holds a three-line headline (top left), a two-line summary, two actions, an advisor line with a small portrait, and the full-width six-station line. The selected station's advice, training and guides sit under it.
