Qualification criteria

Also called qualification requirements

The minimum requirements a bidder must meet to show it can carry out the contract, such as relevant experience, financial capacity, key staff and equipment.

In practice

Qualification is usually assessed pass or fail. A bidder either meets each requirement or it does not, and exceeding a requirement earns no extra credit. Depending on the procedure, qualification is checked before bidding (prequalification) or after evaluation, for the bidder ranked first (post-qualification).

Not the same as evaluation criteria

Evaluation criteria compare the bids that meet the requirements, for example on price, or on a combined score for quality and cost. Qualification asks whether a bidder can do the job; evaluation asks which bid offers the best value.

Qualification criteria compared with evaluation criteria
Aspect Qualification criteria Evaluation criteria
The question Can this bidder carry out the contract? Which compliant bid offers the best value?
Applied as Pass or fail Price, or a score for quality and cost
Where it is set In the bidding document In the bidding document

Terms differ between frameworks

National procurement rules and each financier’s procurement framework can use different terms and procedures. Check the rules that apply to your procurement.
