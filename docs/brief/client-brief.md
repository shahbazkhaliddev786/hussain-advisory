## One feature I would add from the beginning

Create a **"Ask PASA"** section.

Visitors could submit general procurement questions such as:

"What is the difference between qualification and evaluation criteria?"

PASA could select useful questions and publish educational answers in
the Knowledge Hub.

Those answers then become **website content + Facebook posts + Instagram
carousels**. It gives you a continuous source of relevant content while
building PASA\'s reputation as a procurement knowledge resource.

### My recommended Phase 1

Don\'t build everything immediately. For the initial website, launch
with **Home, About, Services, Training, Knowledge Hub, Expertise, and
Contact**. Populate the Knowledge Hub with perhaps 8--12 high-quality
beginner articles/resources before launch. Add advanced features such as
participant accounts, certificates, a full training portal, newsletters,
and extensive resource libraries later.
