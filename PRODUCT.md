## Platform

web

## Stack

Confirmed by the user: Next.js (App Router), Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui, in TypeScript. The site is a single landing page (the user's decision: a portfolio site, not a content platform). Hosting and preview deploys are deferred for now.

## Users

- Public officials and procurement staff in government departments and project implementation units who plan procurements, run bidding and evaluation, and manage contracts.
- Development professionals and project teams on donor-financed projects who must follow a financier's procurement framework.
- Newcomers to procurement (junior staff, people moving into procurement roles) who need plain-language learning.
- Organizations (government departments, development projects, NGOs, procurement teams) looking for advisory support, customized training or capacity assessments.

## Product Purpose

The website presents PASA's procurement advisory, training and mentoring, and grows PASA into a public-procurement learning resource so visitors return instead of visiting once. Success: within a few seconds a visitor understands who PASA is, what PASA does and why to stay, then requests advisory support, registers for training, or keeps learning in the Knowledge Hub.

## Positioning

PASA is the practice of one senior practitioner, Naeem Abbas: more than 30 years in public procurement and contract management, including more than two decades training and mentoring public officials and development professionals. PASA covers the whole procurement cycle (plan, bid, evaluate, award, manage, close) as both advice and teaching, and publishes plain-language knowledge for the profession.

## Operating Context

- The procurement cycle is the profession's shared mental model; services, training topics and Knowledge Hub categories all follow it.
- Everyday material: procurement plans, specifications and terms of reference, bidding documents, notices, clarifications, evaluation reports, contracts, variations and payments.
- Terms and procedures differ between national rules and each financier's procurement framework.
- PASA already publishes educational posts on Facebook and Instagram; website articles become the core content, adapted for social channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Launch scope, as decided by the user: one landing page with hero, services, the procurement cycle, about Naeem, training, FAQ and a contact form. The brief's larger sitemap (inner pages, Knowledge Hub, dictionary) was built once and set aside (closed PR #2); it can return later if PASA wants a content site.
- Homepage, in order: hero; what PASA does; about PASA; meet the advisor; experience; latest three Knowledge Hub resources; upcoming training; call to action.
- Training program pages: overview, learning objectives, who should attend, topics, trainer, duration, delivery method, fee or free, date, venue or online, register.
- Forms: training registration (name, organization, designation, email, phone, training selected, professional background); contact inquiry (name, organization, email, phone, area of interest, message); Ask PASA questions (recommended from launch).
- Admin (later milestone): pages and media, Knowledge Hub content, programs and sessions, registrations, inquiries with New / In progress / Closed status, contact messages, settings.
- Undecided: one canonical service list (the brief has conflicting versions), Procurement Methods as a menu category, payment process for paid training, country or region, domain and professional email, languages.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: PASA, Public Procurement and Supply Advisor.
- Logo: the three-leaf mark (blue #548AD2, yellow #FCDE7E, light blue #72AEE4) with a navy wordmark, as shown in the client's mockup and approved by the user. A vector redraw lives in `brand/`; original artwork not yet supplied.
- Voice: PASA speaks as a practice led by one advisor, Naeem Abbas. No team claims.
- Required wording: "experience with projects financed by…", never implying endorsement by, partnership with, or a client relationship with any financier. Development Partner Procurement is educational. Bid evaluation services are framed around independence, conflicts of interest and applicable rules. Procurement-methods content always notes that terms and procedures differ between frameworks. Case studies are real only with permission, otherwise labelled "Illustrative Procurement Case Study". Gallery photos are genuine PASA photos only.

## Product Principles

1. Teach first: published knowledge builds the trust that leads to advisory work and training.
2. The procurement cycle is the map: every service, program and article knows where it sits in the cycle.
3. Credibility through precision: exact wording, no inflated claims, no implied endorsements.
4. One senior practitioner, not a firm: the advisor's experience is the proof.
5. Plain language that works for beginners and holds up for experts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
